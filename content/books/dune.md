# Dune
**Author:** Frank Herbert

<img src="content/books/dune-cover.jpg" width="200" alt="Dune Book Cover" />

August 6, 2026


It's 4am in the morning. I am looking outside the window of my apartment. Some lights are on. It is supposed to be hot today by Seattle standards. But right now i can feel the cool breeze passing through my window. I can hear the crickets. Street lights are glowing. What would Liet Kynes think of this whole life and the location i am living through. It would be his dream. The one he dreamed for Arrakis. The Arrakis where he dreamed of plants, proper weather patterns with a lot of moisture and the flow of water everywhere. 

I am not going to summarize or restate what has been said in the book. Reading Dune has been a great personal journey for me. This is my second read of the book. During the first read i had trouble understanding the nuances of the characters. Even the names sounded weird and hard to keep in my mind while moving through the book. I then watched the movie. Dune 1 and 2. Both of them were great. A visual treat. I still couldn't make sense of the name and the story to my satisfaction. I thought maybe this kind of writing doesn't resonate with me. Then I listened to a podcast developed based on the book. Gom Jabbar podcast. Those guys clarified so many things for me. What Dune is. What Caladan is. Who the characters. What is the goal. The podcast actually made me reread the book again. Also it became a project for me to finish first three dune books before the release of Dune part 3 movie in December. 

What if you are just a part of a long chain of experiments conducted since millenia. What if the kid you gave birth to was a result of that experiment. I feel for Jessica. She has to respect the school of Bene Gesserit. But at the same time she is a mother who knew whose kid Paul Atreides had to be subjected to torture and upheavals to fulfill some prephecy as prophecized by Missionaria Protectiva. She had to train him to be ready to face what's coming. They wanted to find out if he is the Kwisatch Haderach. The one with the touch of musculine and feminine qualities. The one who is prescient of possibilities.  

"A beginning is the time for taking the most delicate care that the balances are correct. This every sister of Bene Gesserit knows." First two sentences of the book. What does it mean. Think of the preconditions you have set to get to a goal. The school of Bene Gesserit understands it at a deeper level. The eugenics experiment they are conducting very much depends on the balanced settings of the beginning. Something they call in complexity 'sensitive dependence on intial conditions".

You made Arrakis your own after winning over a series of life threatening challenges. You now are in control of the spice. The very thing on which the universe depends. Guild navigators can't navigate without it. Instant communication is blocked when the supply of spice throttled. You are in control of all the spice that could be harvested on Arrakis. What would you do. Paul Atreides ponders. All the great houses, Harkonnens and even the emperor are after you. They all want to rule over Arrakis. They all want that spice to gain power over the whole empire. Paul rises to the occasion and demands he has control over the entire CHOAM company who runs the whole empire business. His goal is to go to the top with the use of spice and Arrakis as his leverage. He wins over Arrakis first. Not with violence. But with learning the ways of the Fremen and the Arrakian world. He rides the sandworm, falls in love with Chani the Arrakean native and also he gives into the Lisan-Al-Gaib myth of that world. He is an outsider but he knows the ways of Arrakis. He is a fremen. He understands Fremen ways of life as if he has been practicing it before. Control the spice control the universe. He gets there and so it begins. The story of Paul Atreides, the one who is prescient of possibilites. He knows there will be a holy war in his name, there will be mass destruction of planets and people. Maybe he understands he can't do much about what is to come. So he chooses to take the throne and rule the empire.
