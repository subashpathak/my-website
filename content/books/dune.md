# Dune
**Author:** Frank Herbert

<img src="content/books/dune-cover.jpg" width="200" alt="Dune Book Cover" />

August 6, 2026


It's 4am in the morning. I am looking outside the window of my apartment. Some lights are on. It is supposed to be hot today by Seattle standards. But right now i can feel the cool breeze passing through my window. I can hear the crickets chirping. Street lights are glowing. What would Liet Kynes think of this whole life and the location i am living through. It would be his dream. The one he dreamed for Arrakis. The Arrakis where dreamed of plants, proper weather patterns with a lot of moisture and the flow of water everywhere. 

Dune is a world of extreme. It is ruthless but brings out the best in you. It finds a way to get into your inner psyche as well. Maybe you have that worm inside which you wanted to conquer. Maybe the Paul Atreides in you will rise someday to ride the inner sandworm and rule the desert within you. And transform the whole system from scratch. Anyways enough of this inner mental analogy. Let's dive into the book deeper.

I am not going to summarize or restate what has been said in the book. Reading Dune has been a great personal journey for me. This is my second read of the book. During the first read i had trouble understanding the nuances of the characters. Even the names sounded weird and hard to keep in my mind while moving through the book. I then watched the movie. Dune 1 and 2. Both of them were great. A visual treat. I still couldn't make sense of the name and the story to my satisfaction. I thought maybe this kind of writing doesn't resonate with me. Then I listened to a podcast developed based on the book. Gom Jabbar podcast. Those guys clarified so many things for me. What Dune is. What Caladan is. Who the characters. What is the goal. The podcast actually made me reread the book again. Also it became a project for me to finish first three dune books before the release of Dune part 3 movie in December. 

What if you are just a part of a long chain of experiments conducted since millenia. What if the kid you gave birth to was a result of that experiment. I feel for Jessica. She has to respect the school of Bene Gesserit. But at the same time she is a mother who knew whose kid Paul Atreides had to be subjected to torture and upheavals to fulfill some prephecy as prophecized by Missionaria Protectiva. She had to train her to be ready to face what's coming. They wanted to find out if he is the Kwisatch Haderach. The one with the touch of musculine and feminine qualities. The one who is prescient of possibilities.  

"A beginning is the time for taking the most delicate care that the balances are correct. This every sister of Bene Gesserit knows." First two sentences of the book.
