# A Brief History of Intelligence
**Author:** Michael Bennett

Re-reading this to better understand the evolution of human intelligence. The author lays out five key breakthroughs that humans as species went through that led to the position we are in. It took millions of years of evolutionary adaptation jumping through these breakthroughs for intelligence to take its current form. 

Five breakthroughs
1. Steering
2. Reinforcement
3. Simulating
4. Mentalizing
5. Speaking
