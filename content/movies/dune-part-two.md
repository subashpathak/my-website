# Dune: Part one and Two

This week I finished rereading Dune 1 and have started with Messiah. I have to read it now. Can't stop. After the reading is over i rewatched dune 1 and dune 2 back to back over the course of past couple of days. 

The first movie was good. Visually stunning. The conception of the world as described in the book in visual form is amazing. Dennis Villanavue knows how to transform books into movies. Comparatively Dune part two was better than one. I watched part one in couple of sittings in instalments. But the second one i finished in the same setting. I thought i would go to bed after watching like an hour of the movie. But it kept me hooked from start till the end. Great movies are always like that. They make you feel like three hours went by just like that. 

Both of these movies are adapted so well in the screen. Villanavue is careful not to distort the overall theme of the book. At the same time he doesn't hesitate to add his own spin to the story. That's why not every events would match the book. But the book's major theme shines well.
