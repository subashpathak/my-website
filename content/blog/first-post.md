# Hello World
*Published: 2026-07-15*

Welcome to my new minimal blog! 

I recently decided to strip away all the complex UI frameworks and heavy JavaScript from my personal site. The goal is to focus entirely on the content: text.

## Why Minimal?

In an era where every website loads megabytes of JavaScript just to render a static article, going back to basics feels like a breath of fresh air. 

* Fast load times
* Easy to maintain
* Markdown-first writing workflow

Thanks for reading!
